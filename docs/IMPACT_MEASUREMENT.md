# Kintsugi business-impact measurement plan

Do not claim savings, recovery rates, reduced handle time, or customer outcomes until measured in a real, authorised pilot. The model below defines what to measure and how to calculate it.

## Primary outcome
**Safe next-step completion rate** = sessions where the customer correctly identifies and confirms the appropriate next action / eligible completed sessions.

## Operational metrics
- Time to first safe action (median seconds)
- Human handoff acceptance rate = accepted handoffs / handoff attempts
- Knowledge answer grounding rate = answers with an approved source ID / knowledge answers
- Unsupported-answer rate = answers not supported by the approved policy corpus / audited answers
- False-completion rate = users who believe an action was completed when no backend confirmation exists / tested users
- Abandonment rate by journey and step
- Repeat-contact rate within an agreed window (requires an authorised pilot and privacy review)

## Financial model
Let:
- N = eligible annual sessions
- d = share of sessions resolved or prepared without an agent (measured)
- m = average human handling minutes avoided (measured against a control or baseline)
- c = fully loaded cost per agent minute (provided by the institution)
- I = annual integration, licensing, operations and governance cost

Estimated gross capacity value = N × d × m × c.

Estimated net annual value = gross capacity value − I.

This is a scenario model, not a revenue or savings claim. Do not count a session as deflected merely because the user left the chat; require a validated outcome and guard against unsafe self-service.

## Pilot design
- Compare a baseline/control journey with the Kintsugi-assisted journey.
- Pre-register the outcome definitions and observation window.
- Segment fraud and hardship; do not combine their outcomes without explanation.
- Audit a sample of answers for policy grounding and harmful overclaiming.
- Include vulnerable-user and accessibility review.
- Obtain institutional privacy, security, legal and compliance approval before processing real customer data.

## Reporting
Show actual sample sizes, confidence intervals where appropriate, missing data, adverse outcomes, and limitations. Keep projected scenarios visually separate from measured results.
