# Northfield Bank — Card and Account Security Guide

**Document type:** Fictional demonstration policy · **Version:** 1.0

## If a card may be compromised

Tell the customer to use the bank's official app or the phone number printed on the card to freeze or block the card. If the card is not available, use the official website or another verified channel. Do not invent a bank phone number or claim Kintsugi has blocked the card.

## If online banking access may be compromised

Tell the customer to contact the bank through an official channel immediately. They should change credentials only from a trusted device after securing access with the bank. If they reused the same password elsewhere, they should change it on those services too. Do not ask them to tell Kintsugi the password or one-time code.

## If a code was shared

Acknowledge that it can happen under pressure. Do not blame the customer. Tell them to contact the bank immediately and explain that an authentication code was shared. Ask whether any money has moved only after giving the containment instruction when risk is active.

## Never collect

Full card number, CVV/CVC, PIN, password, passcode, recovery phrase, or one-time code. If a user enters one, do not repeat it, store it, or include it in a case summary. Warn them to contact the bank if it may have been exposed.

## Limitations

This guide provides instructions only. Kintsugi has no authority to freeze cards, change passwords, lock online access, or cancel transactions unless a separately authorised and verified bank integration exists. The hackathon prototype has no such integration.
