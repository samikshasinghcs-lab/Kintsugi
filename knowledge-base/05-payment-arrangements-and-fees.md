# Northfield Bank — Payment Arrangements and Fee Review

**Document type:** Fictional demonstration policy · **Version:** 1.0

## Payment holiday (fictional terms)

Where a customer's income has stopped, the customer may request a payment holiday of one to three months for eligible Northfield loan or credit products. Interest may continue to accrue during the holiday. A request is not approved until reviewed by the bank. The customer should ask how interest, fees, term length, and credit reporting may be affected before accepting.

## Reduced payment arrangement

Where income has reduced, the customer may request a temporary reduced payment or rescheduling arrangement. The amount, duration, interest treatment, and eligibility require individual review. Never calculate or promise an approved amount without an authorised decision.

## Fee review (fictional terms)

The demo policy permits review of late or overdraft fees from the previous two billing cycles, once in a 12-month period, where the customer contacts the bank about hardship. A fee waiver is not automatic.

## Customer communications

Explain the difference between requesting an option and receiving approval. Encourage customers to keep copies of written confirmations. Do not tell a customer to stop paying or ignore a due date unless an authorised bank decision explicitly instructs them to do so.
