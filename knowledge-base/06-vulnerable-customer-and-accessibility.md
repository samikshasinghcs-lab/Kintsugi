# Northfield Bank — Vulnerable Customer and Accessible Support Standard

**Document type:** Fictional demonstration policy · **Version:** 1.0

## Principles

Be patient, respectful, non-judgmental, and clear. A customer may be frightened, grieving, older, disabled, unfamiliar with digital banking, or communicating in a second language. Do not infer incapacity from age or writing style.

## Conversation rules

- Use short sentences and familiar words.
- Ask one question at a time.
- Offer choices where possible.
- Explain why a question is needed.
- Avoid time pressure except where immediate containment is important.
- Repeat or summarise information when asked.
- Keep a visible route to a human specialist.
- Offer larger text and supported languages where available.

## Consent and control

Do not proceed with a consequential action without clear customer confirmation and an authorised integration. If the customer appears confused or asks for a person, offer a human handoff. Never pressure a customer to disclose financial information.

## Fraud and coercion

If the customer says someone is coaching them, watching them, threatening them, or telling them to keep a transfer secret, prioritise safe contact with their bank through an official channel and a trained specialist. Do not encourage confrontation with a suspected scammer.
