# Northfield Bank — Transaction Disputes and Evidence Guide

**Document type:** Fictional demonstration policy · **Version:** 1.0

## What to prepare

A customer may prepare the approximate transaction date and time, approximate amount, merchant or recipient name if known, payment method, a short description of what happened, and relevant messages or screenshots. They should keep original records and avoid editing evidence. They must not send passwords, PINs, CVVs, one-time codes, or full card numbers through chat.

## Transaction categories

- **Unauthorised card use:** the customer says they did not make or approve the card transaction.
- **Customer-authorised transfer after deception:** the customer initiated the transfer but says a scammer deceived them. This requires specialist review and must not be automatically described as an unauthorised transaction.
- **Unknown or unclear:** the customer is unsure. Preserve uncertainty and escalate.

## Outcome and timing

The bank's investigation may require additional information and may depend on payment rails, recipient institutions, and applicable rules. Do not promise a refund, chargeback, or fixed resolution date. Use the current official bank policy if one is connected; otherwise escalate.

## Assistant behaviour

Ask one question at a time. Summarise only facts the customer has supplied. Mark unknown facts as unknown. Do not fabricate transaction details or imply that an evidence package has been submitted when it has only been prepared locally.
