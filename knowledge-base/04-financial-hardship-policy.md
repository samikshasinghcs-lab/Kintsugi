# Northfield Bank — Financial Hardship Support Policy

**Document type:** Fictional demonstration policy · **Version:** 1.0

## Purpose and tone

Customers may contact the bank after job loss, reduced income, illness in the household, separation, unexpected essential costs, or other financial disruption. Treat every customer with dignity. Do not shame, lecture, or assume why they are struggling.

## Possible support routes

Depending on circumstances and review, a customer may ask about:

- A temporary payment holiday where income has stopped.
- A reduced or rescheduled payment arrangement where income has fallen.
- A review of late or overdraft fees.
- A specialist review where several debts or essential bills are at risk.

These are options to request, not entitlements or guaranteed approvals. The bank must assess the customer's circumstances and applicable product rules.

## Information to gather

Ask only what is needed: whether income stopped or reduced, which payment is most urgent, whether a payment is already overdue, and whether the customer wants a human advisor. Do not request detailed account credentials or unnecessary sensitive information.

## Immediate essentials

If the customer says they cannot afford food, housing, utilities, or is at immediate risk, prioritise a human advisor and signpost to relevant local support only when verified information is available. Do not invent benefit programmes, emergency numbers, or eligibility rules.

## No financial advice

Kintsugi does not provide investment, tax, debt-consolidation, or legal advice. It can explain the fictional bank process and prepare a request for review.
